# Benefit Atlas pilot: recruitment

Invite **five current PSHCP members** who are comfortable testing in English. If possible, include people who rarely look up benefits as well as people who do it regularly. Ask what device they usually use to look up benefits so you can test a mix of phones and computers. No need to ask about their coverage, health, or claims.

## Copy-and-send invitation

**Subject:** Can you help test a PSHCP benefit lookup tool? (25 minutes)

> I’m trying out Benefit Atlas, an independent, English-language prototype that helps people find PSHCP benefit information. I’m looking for five plan members to spend about 25 minutes trying it alongside the public PSHCP coverage guide. This isn’t a test of your benefits knowledge.
>
> We’ll use made-up examples. You won’t need to sign in, file a claim, or share any personal, medical, or plan details. I’ll take brief notes on what’s easy or confusing; no recording. Participation is optional, and you can skip a task or stop at any time.
>
> If you’re interested, please reply with a couple of times that work for you and whether you’d normally look up benefits on a phone or computer. Please don’t send any health or claim information.

## Set up the sessions

- Start with individual invites. Don’t share a public launch announcement or the task prompts in advance.
- Keep scheduling details separate from notes. Label notes `P01`–`P05`; record what happened, not who said it. Don’t collect or commit participant notes in this repository.
- Use the device the member normally uses. Run `npm start` on the test device and open `http://127.0.0.1:4173/`. A local URL is only reachable on that device. For remote sessions without a private preview, let the member control the moderator’s shared browser if possible; don’t substitute a moderator-led demo for a timed hands-on task.
