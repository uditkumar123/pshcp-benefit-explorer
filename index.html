How to submit a claim

01

Check the details

Open a benefit and review the eligible expense limit, when it resets, and the documents or approvals it may require.

02

Check your actual coverage

Sign in to Canada Life or MSH to check your balance and reasonable and customary charges before spending.

03

Submit to the right place

Most claims go to Canada Life. MSH handles travel emergencies and expenses incurred outside Canada under Comprehensive Coverage. Use the table below.

Where to submit a claim

Deadlines and emergency help

Claims must reach the administrator by December 31 of the year following the year the expense was incurred. Emergency travel assistance is available 24 hours a day: North America toll-free 1-833-774-2700; international collect 1-365-337-7427.
