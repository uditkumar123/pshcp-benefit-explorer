A LITTLE CONTEXT GOES A LONG WAY

What the numbers actually mean.

01 / THE LIMIT

Eligible expense ≠ payout

A $500 limit with 80% reimbursement means up to $400 reimbursed on $500 of eligible expenses—not $500 back.

02 / YOUR PROVIDER

Actual rates can vary

Claims use reasonable and customary charges, location, provider eligibility and plan exclusions. Your receipt may be higher than the amount considered eligible.

03 / OTHER PLANS

Order of claims matters

Provincial or territorial coverage and other benefit plans can affect payment. Coordination of benefits can reimburse up to 100% of eligible expenses.

CLAIMING MADE SIMPLER

Where do you submit a claim?

For most regular non-emergency claims, start with Canada Life. For emergency travel assistance under Supplementary Coverage or qualifying expenses incurred outside Canada under Comprehensive Coverage, use MSH.

Always complete positive enrolment first. The usual claim deadline is December 31 of the year after the expense was incurred (exceptions may apply).
