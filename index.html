WHAT HAPPENS NEXT?

From looking up a benefit to making a claim.

01

Check the details

Open a benefit and review the eligible expense limit, timing, and documents or approvals it may require.

02

Check your actual coverage

Sign in to Canada Life to check your balance and reasonable and customary charges before spending.

03

Submit to the right place

Most regular claims go to Canada Life. MSH handles emergency travel assistance and Comprehensive Coverage claims.
